# Copyright & approved material use

Paramedic Study Buddy is a **personal / cohort study aid**. It is not a publisher, bookstore, or content redistribution service. We respect authors’ and publishers’ rights and only use copyrighted material in ways that are licensed or otherwise permitted.

## Principles

1. **No piracy.** Do not upload, scrape, or share unauthorized copies of textbooks, exam banks, or publisher PDFs.
2. **License first.** Only ingest material the student or program has a right to use (enrolled course pack, instructor-provided files, personally purchased titles under that license’s terms).
3. **Private study scope.** Materials and RAG indexes are owner- or class-library scoped for enrolled classmates—not public redistribution.
4. **Attribution.** Third-party titles remain the property of their authors and publishers; Study Buddy does not claim ownership of those works.
5. **Study aid disclaimer.** Generated practice items and AI feedback are for learning only—not official NREMT, publisher, or academy exam content.
6. **No DRM bypass.** Do not strip DRM, print-restricted eBooks, or “export” reader apps into a full PDF for ingest. That violates publisher terms and U.S. copyright law.

## Course / academy materials

St. Anthony Paramedic Academy Cycle materials, Navigate2 / Ascend Learning–related course files, and instructor handouts are used for **private study by enrolled students**, consistent with program access. They must not be shared outside the authorized cohort.

### What “buy eBook” does *not* authorize

MedMaster’s [terms](https://medmaster.net/pages/terms-and-conditions) and [FAQ](https://medmaster.net/pages/faq) state that eBooks:

- Cannot be printed.
- May not be systematically retrieved to build a collection, compilation, **database**, or directory **without prior written permission**.

So a consumer eBook purchase is for **reading and personal study in their reader**, not for bulk-exporting the full text into Study Buddy’s class library or personal RAG index.

| Goal                                               | Approved approach                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read the book now                                  | Buy MedMaster eBook or VitalSource; study in their app/web reader.                                                      |
| Practice in Study Buddy on *academy* pharm content | Use already-authorized Cycle materials already in the corpus.                                                           |
| Full-text embed / RAG over Olson                   | Contact MedMaster for written educational / multi-user / text-mining permission *before* any ingest. Do not bypass DRM. |
| Shared class library                               | Only if the license explicitly allows multi-user or institutional use.                                                  |

**Contact for permissions:** use MedMaster site support / sales (medmaster.net) and request written authorization for educational RAG or cohort use. Keep that email with the project records.

## Student learning profile (privacy)

Preferred name, study preferences, self-reported weak topics, and check-ins live in Amplify as **owner-private** `StudentProfile` / `StudyCheckIn` rows. Classmates cannot read them. A short summary may be sent with Tutor requests to xAI **for that reply only** (no long-term xAI profile; email never included). Clear learning profile in Settings anytime; practice Attempts are kept separately.
