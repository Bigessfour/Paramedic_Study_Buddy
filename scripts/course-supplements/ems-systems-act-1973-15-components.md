# Supplement: EMS Systems Act of 1973 — 15 required components

_Course-aligned study aid. Chapter lecture outlines/slides refer to **Table 1-1** for these components but the table cells are often not present in extractable DOCX/PDF text. This list matches the standard EMS Systems Act (1973) enumeration taught with Nancy Caroline / AAOS EMS Systems (Chapter 1)._

1. Manpower
2. Training
3. Communications
4. Transportation
5. Facilities
6. Critical care units
7. Public safety agencies
8. Consumer participation
9. Access to care
10. Patient transfer
11. Standardized record-keeping
12. Public information and education
13. System review and evaluation
14. Disaster planning
15. Mutual aid

Also commonly contrasted in the same chapter: **NHTSA 10 system elements** used to help sustain EMS systems (distinct from the 15 legislative components above). When the extract only names “10 system elements” without listing them, review Table 1-1 / the textbook figure in Chapter 1.
